What is Dream 11

Dream11 is an Indian fantasy sports platform where users can create virtual teams of real-life players from various sports, such as cricket, football, basketball, kabaddi, and more. These virtual teams earn points based on the performance of the real players in actual matches. Users compete against each other, and the teams with the highest points win prizes. Dream11 is popular for its focus on cricket, particularly during major tournaments like the Indian Premier League (IPL).

The platform offers both free and paid contests, with paid contests offering cash prizes. Dream11 uses a freemium model, where users can join free contests or pay an entry fee to join premium contests with higher rewards. The success of Dream11 has inspired the growth of fantasy sports in India, making it a leading player in this industry.
